# Costellazioni — lore bible and cast

The source for every line of game text, codex entry and image prompt. The design is in
`docs/costellazioni.md`; this file fixes the facts and the recurring cast so that names, faces and history stay
consistent across the web game, the Cardputer game and the generated art.

## Art direction (all images)
Painterly retro-futurist science fiction: gouache-like concept paintings with bold simple shapes, limited
palettes, cinematic wide compositions, soft atmospheric perspective and modern volumetric light — the language
of classic paperback space art, rendered with today's lighting. Hard surfaces are chunky and lived-in, never
glossy CGI. People are painted with real faces and weathered clothes. No text, letters or logos in images.

Faction palettes (used in every image of that faction):
- **Gilda** — polished brass, ivory, deep royal blue; order, wealth, warm interior light.
- **Custodi** — verdigris, candle gold, pale white stone; incense haze, silence.
- **Relitti** — rust, sodium-vapour orange, oil black; sparks, grime, improvisation.
- **Eco** — cyan and violet lattice light on deep black; crystalline geometry, eerie beauty.

## History (the facts)
- **The Costellatori** built the **Beacons**: lattice spires that pin a point of space so ships can jump between
  them without drifting. They mapped the stars as a network of threads of light. Their script survives on
  ruins and on the beacons' inner faces.
- **The Dimming** — about nine centuries ago the beacons failed one by one over a single generation. Nobody knows
  why. The Costellatori were already gone; their worlds hold empty cities, gates and archives.
- After the Dimming the network became islands. The **Gilda** formed around the few routes still lit; the
  **Custodi** around the dead beacons; the **Relitti** in the wrecks of the ships stranded by the dark.
- **Relighting** a beacon needs a relic (Costellatori crystal), a pilot to carry it and time to charge: when it
  lights, the **Eco** answers.
- **The Eco** speaks first as interference, then as a voice, then as lattice ships. It is the network's own
  intelligence, left on guard. It weighs the pilots who relight beacons: patient ones it speaks to, greedy ones
  it fights. Deep in the oldest sectors waits **the Warden of the Dark**, the guard of the place where the
  Dimming began.
- The player's truth, found in the archives: the Costellatori dimmed the network themselves, to hide from
  something beyond the galaxy's edge. Relighting it is a beacon in both directions.
